# Demo video: 75 to 90 seconds

**Format:** vertical iPhone screen recording of real Messages threads, with a
voice-over recorded afterwards (or captions). Two phones: Jack's (founder)
and a second phone as "Dana", the customer champion. Priya (engineer) can be a
third phone or a teammate. Use a fictional but realistic company (Loopline)
and customers; do not show real customer data or phone numbers (blur them).

**Prep (before recording):** fresh state (`docker compose down -v`, or a new
cloud line). Have the customer notes and FAQ ready to paste. Do one dry run.
Messages arrive in seconds but not instantly, so cut the waits in editing and
never fake a reply.
If 1-click deploy isn't enabled yet when you record, start at Casey's first
reply on your own test line and show the install line as a caption instead of
texting it.

## Shot list

1. Title card (logo on teal, name, one-liner). Make it in Keynote or Canva, 3 to 6 s.
2. Founder phone: install text and Casey's intro.
3. Founder phone: onboarding answers, account cards.
4. Founder phone: first morning brief (hold 3 s so it's readable; zoom in editing).
5. Founder phone: thread request, opener draft, `yes 2`.
6. Dana phone: group thread with opener, FAQ answer, bug report, Casey's @mention of Priya.
7. Dana phone: discount request, Casey's holding reply.
8. Founder phone: approval item A-3, edit, `yes 3`.
9. Dana phone: approved reply lands, then the summary on request.
10. Founder phone: weekly report and renewal pack (can be triggered on demand with `weekly report` and `prep acme`).
11. End card with the install line.

## Editing notes

- Keep it at least 60 s (rule) and aim for 85 s.
- Captions on for every message that matters; phone text is small on YouTube.
- Blur phone numbers and the Plow line number if you prefer.
- Grab screenshots for the Index from shots 4, 6, 8 and 9 while editing.
- Upload to YouTube (Public or Unlisted, embedding allowed) and use the video
  ID (not the URL) in the listing.
